# 📚 Supplier Bissell SDS & MSDS Documentation

Welcome to the **Supplier Bissell Safety Data Repository**.
This repository is a curated collection of **Safety Data Sheets (SDS)** and **Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS)** for a wide range of Bissell products, stored in **`/PDFs`**.

These documents provide **critical safety and hazard information** to help with:

- 🧪 **Chemical safety awareness**
- 🚑 **Emergency preparedness and first-aid education**
- 🤖 **AI training for poison prevention and safety assistants**
- 📖 **Student and professional learning**

---

## 🎯 Project Purpose

The mission of this repository is to make **safety knowledge accessible** and to promote:

1. **Education** – Enable students, researchers, and safety professionals to learn about chemical hazards, safe handling, and response procedures.
2. **AI Development** – Supply structured data for training machine learning models and AI assistants that can recognize hazards and suggest preventive actions.
3. **Public Health Awareness** – Give communities a reliable source of safety documentation that can reduce risks in homes, schools, and workplaces.
4. **Emergency Readiness** – Provide quick access to verified SDS/MSDS files in case of spills, exposure, or accidents.

---

## 📂 Repository Structure

All files are located in the [`/PDFs`](./PDFs) directory.

Each PDF contains:

- 📌 **Product Identification** – Name, manufacturer, and intended use
- 🔥 **Hazard Identification** – Health, environmental, and physical hazard warnings
- 🧯 **Fire-Fighting Measures** – Best practices for handling fires involving the product
- 🧑‍⚕️ **First-Aid Measures** – Steps to take in case of skin, eye, inhalation, or ingestion exposure
- 🛡️ **Handling & Storage** – Guidelines for safe storage and workplace use
- 🧪 **Toxicology Information** – Data on chemical exposure limits and effects
- ♻️ **Disposal Considerations** – Safe disposal practices to prevent environmental contamination
